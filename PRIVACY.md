# Privacy Policy — Simple Calendar Widget

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

Simple Calendar Widget does not collect, store, or share any personal information
with its developer or anyone else. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

## What the app stores

The calendar links you add, and your settings (theme, pin position), are saved only on
your own computer, in `%APPDATA%\Calendar Widget\config.json`. They are never sent to
the developer. Uninstalling the app or deleting that file removes them.

## What the app connects to

- **Your calendar provider** (for example Apple iCloud or Google). The app downloads
  your calendar from the links you add, directly from that provider, every 15 minutes.
  The provider's own privacy policy applies to that connection.
- **Google Fonts.** The app loads its font from Google's servers, which receive your
  IP address as part of the request. See
  [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The app does not connect to any other server.
